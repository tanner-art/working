# Threadline Friends-and-Family Beta Privacy Notice

**Status:** Beta — this product is in active development and is not production software.

## What This Beta Does

Threadline is a thought-to-execution system. During this beta, you can:

- Capture text and voice notes (we call these "captures")
- Add them to an infinite visual canvas
- Create actions, commitments, ideas, and projects
- Review and organize your thinking
- Save and sync your work across signed-in devices

## What You Enter and Where It Goes

**Your captures are personal.** Text, voice recordings, canvas notes, and any content you add stay yours. This is a beta; we're learning how the system works and may need to ask you about your experience.

### Local-Only Mode
When you use Threadline without signing in, everything stays on your device. Your browser stores your captures locally. We cannot see them, and they do not leave your phone or computer.

### Signed-In Mode
When you sign in with an email address, Threadline syncs your captures and work between your devices. Only your devices can access your data — our backend applies row-level security rules to ensure account isolation.

## AI Interpretation

Threadline can interpret your captures to suggest actions, ideas, and topics. This is optional and off by default.

**When you enable AI interpretation:**
- Only the single capture you are interpreting is sent to our AI service.
- The AI reads it, suggests meaning, and sends back a proposal.
- Proposals appear for your review; they do not automatically become actions or commitments.
- You see everything the AI suggested, decide whether to accept it, and can edit or reject it.

**The AI does not:**
- Access your other captures, history, or work
- Take action on its own
- Create obligations without your explicit confirmation

## Proposals Stay Review-Only

All AI-suggested meanings — actions, commitments, ideas — begin as proposals in your Review section. They do not affect your calendar, task list, or commitment count until you explicitly confirm each one.

## Voice Audio Expiration

If you capture voice recordings during the beta:
- Raw audio is kept for 14 days by default
- After 14 days, the recording is automatically deleted, but the transcript and any meaning you confirmed remains
- You can save a recording to keep it indefinitely
- You can change the retention setting in your account

Transcripts and confirmed interpretations are not deleted when audio expires.

## Diagnostics and Feedback

To help us improve Threadline, we may collect:
- Crash reports and error logs
- Usage patterns (which features you use)
- Feature feedback you voluntarily share

**By default, diagnostics exclude:**
- Your captures, canvas content, or any personal information you entered
- Your identity (account details beyond what we need to identify you as a tester)
- Voice audio or its transcript

You can choose to include or exclude diagnostic collection in your settings.

## Rankings and Opt-In Analytics

Threadline plans to show you personal statistics about your own work — how many ideas you captured, completion rates, and similar insights.

Cross-user rankings or comparisons (for example, "fastest capturer in the group") require your explicit opt-in. They are not enabled during beta, and we will ask for your consent before showing them. You can opt out at any time without losing access to your personal statistics.

## Your Choices During Beta

You control:

- **What data syncs.** Turn off sync at any time; local-only mode remains available.
- **AI interpretation.** Keep it off, enable it for specific captures, or turn it on for all new captures.
- **Diagnostic sharing.** Choose whether error reports and usage data are sent.
- **Audio retention.** Save individual recordings or change your default expiration window.
- **Feedback.** Volunteer feedback or stay silent; it is entirely optional.

## Avoiding Highly Sensitive Material During Beta

This is a beta. Data storage and account isolation are not yet production-grade. To be safe:

- Avoid entering passwords, API keys, or other secrets
- Don't store highly sensitive personal, medical, or financial information
- Don't share access to this beta account with others unless you trust them with your work
- If you feel unsure whether something is safe to enter, err on the side of caution

## Before You Share a Diagnostic Report

If you report an error or issue:

1. **Review what will be included.** The diagnostic report will show you what data it contains before you send it.
2. **Remove sensitive details if needed.** Edit the report to remove any captures or examples you don't want to share, then submit.
3. **We'll ask follow-up questions separately** if we need more context — we won't ask you to re-send more data than necessary.

## Data and Deletion

During this beta:
- Local data stays on your device. Deleting the app or clearing your browser storage removes it.
- Synced data in our database is associated with your email address.
- Signing out stops syncing but does not delete your account data.
- We do not yet have a formal deletion process; that will be implemented before moving to production.

**We do not guarantee** data deletion, backups, or recovery during this beta phase. Treat your beta data as experimental.

## Security and Compliance

This is beta software, not production-grade. We have not undergone security audits or compliance certification. Do not enter data that requires production-level security or regulatory compliance guarantees.

We are learning what security and privacy safeguards matter most to you and refining them based on feedback.
